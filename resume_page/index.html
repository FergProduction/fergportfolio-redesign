Process / Workflow

Present

Personal Pipline

My workflow is reference driven from start to finish, with a strong focus on presentation and polish. I typically model and animate using industry-standard tools, texture in Substance Painter, and bring everything into Unreal for lighting, rendering, and real-time effects. A large part of my process happens in post-production, where I refine lighting, color, and overall presentation in After Effects, while staying comfortable adapting across different software, pipelines, and technical needs depending on the project.

Full Creator

I modeled and designed the character and assets using 3ds Max and Marvelous Designer, combined mocap animation with hands on editing, and brought everything together in Unreal. This project marked my first time fully blending 3D production with my video editing workflow into a single, polished cinematic piece.
