PermaFrost

Permafrost is a modern survival horror game inspired by the atmosphere driven horror of the late 1990s, when isolation, limited resources, and fear shaped the experience. Trapped inside a frozen, isolated facility, players fight through escalating threats where fear, limited resources, and evolving enemies stand between survival and escape. As the storm closes in and the world collapses, the game builds toward a final stand where escape comes just before everything is lost.
